Bank Risk Scanner

XGBoost + SHAP interaction model trained on 20 years of FDIC call report data (2005–2024) · 570k bank-quarters · 2,228 failure events

What this is: A machine learning model trained to detect bank failure signatures 12 months in advance, using quarterly FDIC call report data. Features are engineered as multiplicative interactions — the combinations that actually kill banks (thin capital × rising NPLs, uninsured deposits × securities portfolio, etc.). The model has AUC 0.953 in time-series cross-validation and 42.6× lift over base rate when combining risk score + velocity signals.

🔴 SVB Signature
securities_ratio × uninsured_dep_ratio

High securities portfolio (duration risk) funded by flighty uninsured deposits. Becomes lethal when rates rise — unrealized losses meet a runnable liability base. Heritage Bank KY has the highest current score (0.078).
🔴 Run Risk
uninsured_dep_ratio × (1 − tier1/20%)

Pure liquidity run risk: high uninsured deposits combined with insufficient capital buffer. When depositors can move $250k+ instantly, thin capital means no room for error.
🔴 Capital × Loan Stress
(1 − equity_ratio) × noncurrent_ratio

The 2008 signature: bad loans absorbing thin capital. Banks with >2% NPL and Tier 1 below 8% have essentially no loss-absorption runway.
🔴 Zombie Bank
leverage × (1/|ROA|)

High leverage combined with near-zero or negative profitability. The bank is burning capital while carrying outsized risk. Fieldpoint and Southpoint both exhibit this pattern severely.

Architecture

Data Pipeline
Source: FDIC BankFind API (public, no auth)
Coverage: All US banks, Q1 2005 – Q4 2024
Records: 570,000 bank-quarters
Failure labels: 2,228 positive events (12-month forward window)
Key fields: ASSET, EQ, LNLSNET, DEPNIDOM, FREPO, SC, NTLNLSR, RBC1AAJ, ROA, NIMY, NONIX
Model
Algorithm: XGBoost (gradient boosted trees)
Features: 20 engineered ratios + interaction terms
Validation: 5-fold time-series cross-validation
Explainability: SHAP TreeExplainer (feature + interaction values)
Clustering: K-means on SHAP space → 4 risk archetypes
