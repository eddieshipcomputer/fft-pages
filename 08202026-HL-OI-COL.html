Who Killed the Funding Rates?
A $40M Wallet and Six Simultaneous Collapses

On August 20, 2026 at 06:00 UTC, annualized funding rates for six Hyperliquid perpetual contracts — CASHCAT, ACE, WLFI, NIL, ATOM, and BOME — all collapsed to exactly 0.00125%/8h within the same 60-minute window. That figure is Hyperliquid's hardcoded protocol floor. Six unrelated assets don't snap to the same constant simultaneously by chance. This report traces the cause to a single on-chain address.

What Happened

A single address — 0xecb63caa47c7c4e77f60f1ce858cf28dc2b82b00 — operates a professional funding arbitrage book with $40M in account value across 83 open positions on Hyperliquid. Their strategy is mechanical: short assets with elevated positive funding, long assets with negative funding, collect both sides of the yield spread.

When this wallet entered a $929k CASHCAT short and accumulated offsetting positions across the affected assets, they became the primary counterparty to the existing long book. As those longs closed — systematically, in block trades spread 20–40 minutes apart — open interest compressed and the funding rate for each asset snapped to the 11% protocol floor. The execution pattern across six assets over 90 minutes is not consistent with organic market behavior. It is consistent with an algorithmic portfolio exit.

The capital did not disappear. Concurrent with the exits on CASHCAT, ACE, and WLFI, BOME saw a 41× volume spike and sustained price appreciation — the rotation destination.

Key Finding

The 11% funding floor (0.00125%/8h) is a Hyperliquid protocol constant, not a market price. For six assets to hit exactly this value within the same hour requires a single entity systematically reducing open interest across all six simultaneously. The on-chain evidence points to one wallet.

Why This Wallet Moved the Market

When 0xecb6 entered a $929k CASHCAT short, they absorbed supply from existing longs. As those longs exited their funding yield trades — the position becoming less attractive once a large counterparty entered — open interest compressed. Each departure of a long position reduces the imbalance between longs and shorts, which is the sole mechanism driving funding above the floor. By the time enough longs had exited, six assets simultaneously hit 0.00125%/8h.

Minute-Level Volume Forensics

The execution signature is the clearest evidence of systematic rather than organic behavior. Block trades appear as discrete spikes — 18× average volume on CASHCAT at 06:53 UTC, 7.7× on ACE at 06:20 UTC, 6× on WLFI at 07:00 UTC — spread 20–40 minutes apart across different assets. This is the pattern of an algorithmic order router working through a position book sequentially, not a human manually panic-selling six tokens.

CASHCAT alone shows three distinct blocks: 06:53 (18×, 556k units), 07:31 (9×, 278k units), and 07:59 (7.5×, 229k units). The 38-minute then 28-minute spacing is consistent with a TWAP execution splitting a large position into three tranches to minimize market impact. Total exit across the three blocks: approximately 1,063,000 units.

BOME: The Rotation Destination

While five assets experienced exit volume, BOME moved in the opposite direction. Its price rose from $0.000877 to $0.001013 during the window — a 15% move — concurrent with a 41× volume spike. This rules out the collapse being driven by a generalized risk-off event; if it were, BOME would have fallen too. Instead, capital exited one set of funding yield trades and rotated into a new one where BOME's funding rate remained elevated.

05 · Takeaways

What This Means for Funding Traders

The Epoch Reset is the Risk Window

Hyperliquid's 8-hour epochs reset at 00:00, 08:00, and 16:00 UTC. Funding arbitrageurs collect at each epoch boundary and may rebalance their books immediately after. Elevated funding positions held through the 16:00 UTC epoch are most exposed — the 00:00 UTC reset (8 PM US Eastern) is prime rebalancing time, and the 06:00 UTC window this report covers was two epochs later.

Six Assets at the Floor Simultaneously Is a Signal, Not a Coincidence

The 11% floor is not a market equilibrium — it's a protocol constant. When multiple unrelated assets hit it in the same hour, a single entity has compressed OI across all of them. This is reproducible: watch for simultaneous floor-snaps across correlated meme coins as a leading indicator of a large portfolio exit.

The Rotation Trade

When a funding collapse occurs, the exiting capital goes somewhere. BOME's 41× volume spike was visible in the public order book in real time. Monitoring for simultaneous above-average volume across meme coins during a funding collapse on related assets is a tractable rotation-detection strategy. The entry window was roughly 60–90 minutes after the initial collapse.

0xecb6 as a Signal Wallet

This address is publicly queryable via Hyperliquid's API. Their position book is a real-time snapshot of where a professional funding arb fund sees yield opportunities. Their CASHCAT short entry preceded the funding compression. Any new large short entries by this wallet in a high-funding meme coin should be treated as a signal that the funding thesis for that asset is under pressure.
